    FAQ

    Good to know

    Is this a medical diagnosis?

    No. It's a statistical estimate based on survey patterns and cannot assess any individual's health. It is not a substitute for professional advice.

    Is my data stored?

    No. Inputs are sent to the server only to compute the prediction and are not saved or logged by this app.

    How accurate is it?

    On held-out test data the model explains about 86% of the variance with an average error of roughly 0.37 points on the 10-point scale.

    I'm struggling — what should I do?

    Please talk to someone you trust or a qualified professional. If you're in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency number. In India, Tele-MANAS offers free 24/7 support at 14416 (or 1-800-891-4416).
